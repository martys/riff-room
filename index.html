Connect the piano

Turn the Kawai on, then connect it one of these ways. Afterwards tap Try again.

USB cable (most reliable)
Plug the piano's USB to Host port (square type B) into the laptop or an Android tablet (with a USB-C adapter). Nothing to pair.
Bluetooth on a Mac
Open Audio MIDI Setup, choose Window → Show MIDI Studio, click the Bluetooth icon and connect the piano. Then use Chrome.
Bluetooth on Android
Bluetooth MIDI usually has to be connected by an app first, such as a "BLE MIDI connect" app or Kawai's PianoRemote. Then open this site in Chrome. Don't pair it in the normal Bluetooth settings.
Windows
Chrome on Windows generally can't use Bluetooth MIDI. Use the USB cable.
iPad and iPhone
No iPad browser can do MIDI, so use a free MIDI browser app from the App Store. Pair the piano inside that app's Bluetooth MIDI screen (not in the iPad's Bluetooth settings), close Kawai's PianoRemote, and make sure Bluetooth MIDI is on in the piano. On an older iPad: Web MIDI Browser (works from iOS 9). On iPadOS 17.6 or newer: MIDIWeb Browser. Open the Riff Room address inside the app, connect the piano with the app's Bluetooth MIDI button, then tap Connect piano here. A USB cable through Apple's camera adapter also works.

Also check that Bluetooth MIDI is switched on in the piano's settings. The app stays silent for piano notes, since the piano makes its own sound.
